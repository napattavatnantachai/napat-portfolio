An 3D film introducing the life of the Buddha, showing his journey to enlightenment. This was one of the projects I contributed to during my time at Undeclared Variable.

Life of the Buddha
This project involved creating an animation of the Buddha’s life, depicting how an ordinary human overcame afflictions and attained enlightenment. Developed using Unreal Engine 5, I contributed to:

  • Character creation

  • Fabric simulation

  • Texturing

  • Prop development
     

About : Fo Guang Shan Buddha Museum
Fo Guang Shan Buddha Museum is one of the largest Buddhist cultural complexes in the world, home to the 108-meter Fo Guang Big Buddha. Located in Kaohsiung, Taiwan, the museum blends tradition and modernity, serving as a center for culture, education, and spiritual cultivation, while enshrining the Buddha’s tooth relic as a symbol of compassion and wisdom.
